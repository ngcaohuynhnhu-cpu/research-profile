01 / About

Research in Sustainability Governance and Regional Economic Development

I am currently a master’s student in Cohort 35.1 at University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH), pursuing the program in Corporate Sustainability and Environmental Management. My research background spans organizational management and tourist behavior, as well as digital transformation, agricultural transitions, and distributional equity in the Mekong Delta.

My evolving research focus is on regional economic development, regional coordination and governance, sustainable livelihoods, access to technology, and the participation of local stakeholders, with an emphasis on evidence-based research to inform policymaking and regional development.

Long-term goal: to connect academic research with practical challenges in Ben Tre, Vinh Long, and the wider Mekong Delta region.

2024Sustainability campaign
SASCO • Ho Chi Minh City
Corporate green initiative implemented in practiceImplemented

Nurturing Small Seeds, Growing Green Aspirations

This campaign was implemented by SASCO in September 2024 across retail and food-and-beverage services at Tan Son Nhat International Airport to encourage green lifestyles and sustainable consumption. Activities included plantable cards made from recycled paper, potted plants, environmentally friendly bags, and interactive experiences such as “The Green Living Journey”.

Role: Nguyen Cao Huynh Nhu proposed the original initiativeand won First Prize in the 2024 “SASCO – Voice of the Pioneers – Youth Aspirations” competition. SASCO subsequently implemented the idea across its airport retail and food-and-beverage operations, using plantable recycled-paper cards, green ceramic planters, and linen bags to encourage sustainable consumption. The award and implementation were featured by Thanh Nien newspaper (15 October 2024) in the article “The Journey of a Seed from Tan Son Nhat Airport to Every Corner of the Country”.

Classification: practical sustainable-consumption and communication project; not a scientific research project or peer-reviewed paper.
